RYAN ENERGY v15 - FIVE DAY DEMO STABILIZATION

1. Extract ZIP to Desktop. Do not run inside the ZIP.
2. Double-click START_RYAN_ENERGY.bat (requires the existing Notebook Python environment).
3. Choose Country > Province > City.
4. Enter Solar PV or Wind Farm design (at least one).
5. Enter demand and economic assumptions.
6. Go to Simulation > Simulation and click Run Complete Simulation.
7. For no internet ONLY: check Offline demonstration in sidebar. All outputs are
   synthetic-weather demonstration, NOT validated engineering data.
8. Save CSV reports before the presentation.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS: EnergyPlus/WaterTAP native solvers not integrated; simplified
greenhouse, desalination and carbon models; fuel/heat not fully sector-coupled.
Do not promise full scientific validation. First full Streamlit/PyPSA run on the
user's Windows laptop still needs to be checked.
