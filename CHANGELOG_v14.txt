RYAN ENERGY v14
- Main navigation: Demand, Supply, Economic Analysis, Simulation.
- Supply contains Solar PV, Wind Farm, Greenhouse, Desalination, Storage & Grid.
- Demand/Fuels includes natural gas and gasoline, diesel, heavy fuel oil, kerosene,
  LPG, jet fuel, crude oil with separate energy quantities.
- Simulation consolidates supply, demand, hourly dispatch, economics, and CO2
  by fuel and grid source, with editable emission factors and CSV download.
- CO2 calculation is direct combustion plus purchased grid power. Factors are
  approximate LHV screening values, not verified national inventory factors.
- Fuel, process heat, and transport fuel are NOT yet dispatched as part of a
  full sector-coupled energy model. Aggregate transport fuel is excluded from
  emissions to avoid guessing fuel mix or double counting.
- Economics recomputes from saved assumptions and current dispatch.
- WaterTAP/EnergyPlus execution still not validated; screening models remain.
- Needs live Windows/Streamlit integration test; Python syntax and isolated
  emissions/economics arithmetic checked.
